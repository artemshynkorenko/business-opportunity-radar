# Business Opportunity Radar — Project Definition v2

## Purpose
Build a private, personal Business Opportunity Radar that discovers high-value situations from public online conversations and helps the user identify people, businesses, partnerships, market-entry opportunities, sourcing opportunities, automation opportunities, projects, and emerging trends.

Core funnel: **signal → conversation → relationship → opportunity**

Optimize for useful opportunities, not content volume.

## User priorities
1. Become partner in an existing business
2. International trade / distribution
3. Businesses entering new markets
4. Partnerships / JVs
5. Interesting business ideas
6. Automation opportunities
7. Manufacturers / suppliers
8. Entrepreneurial projects where help is needed
9. Emerging trends
10. Cofounder / business partner

## Geography
Thailand 10, Russia/CIS 10, EU 8, SEA 8, USA 7, rest 5. Geography is a multiplier, not a hard filter.

## User capabilities
- EU market access
- Thailand access
- Russia/CIS access
- US market access
- Czech export/import company
- US LLC
- entrepreneurial experience
- project launch / organization ability
- international business capability
- remote work capability
- automation / n8n interest

## Strong targets
Established businesses with traction and concrete needs; Thai manufacturers seeking EU expansion; factory owners seeking successor/partner; SEA SaaS founders seeking Thai market entry; businesses with operational pain suitable for automation; local Chiang Mai/Thailand projects; international trade/distribution; remote entrepreneurial projects.

## Exclusions
Infobusiness, MLM, crypto/Web3 as the main topic, career/job seekers, motivational content, political content, generic networking, get-rich schemes, generic AI hype, generic SaaS announcements, startups with no traction and no concrete need, generic cofounder requests without business context.

## Primary analytical object
The primary object is a **Situation**, not a post. Content is evidence. A Situation may combine multiple source contents, comments, author history, and related signals.

## First milestone
Produce 50–100 high-quality situations and manually evaluate whether they are genuinely useful. Do not build the full production system before this quality gate.
