# Search Strategy v2

## Layers
1. Community discovery
2. High-signal keyword discovery
3. Semantic intent detection
4. Author context
5. Cross-content aggregation
6. Trend detection

## High-signal clusters
Partner: looking for business/strategic/operating partner, partner to expand, someone to help scale.

Market entry: entering Thailand, expanding to Europe/EU/SEA/US, local partner, local representation.

Distribution: distributor, reseller, dealer, sales agent, distribution partner.

Manufacturing/sourcing: manufacturer, OEM, ODM, contract manufacturing, private label, reliable supplier, failed supplier, quality/MOQ/lead-time problem.

Automation: manual repetitive work, spreadsheets, copy-paste, manual data entry, admin, CRM, invoicing, inventory, reporting, onboarding. n8n is a capability, not an opportunity category.

Acquisition/succession: sell, buyer, succession, retiring, take over, operating partner, acquire SMB.

Project/operator: build, launch, develop, project partner, operator, take over operations.

## Noise
get rich, passive income, side hustle, MLM, motivational, crypto/memecoin, trading signals, generic AI hype, million-dollar-idea posts, generic cofounder requests without traction.

## Languages
English, Russian, Thai. Generate native queries where possible.

## Critical rule
Score the underlying situation, not the presence of a keyword.
